● I wrote the PRD to context/prd.md. It contains 9 user stories and 16
  functional requirements. FR-01 to FR-15 are Must and FR-16 (demo seed data) is
  Could. Every brief item, numbered B-1…B-7h plus objective, notes, assessment
  and delivery items, has a row in the traceability table.

  I made two decisions the shape notes had left for this step:
  - Error codes (Q-03): BAD_USER_INPUT, PROPERTY_ALREADY_EXISTS,
    PROPERTY_NOT_FOUND, WEATHER_QUOTA_EXCEEDED, WEATHER_UNAVAILABLE,
    WEATHER_LOCATION_MISMATCH and INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR. Each has a trigger and
    the meaning its message must carry (FR-10).
  - Weather fields on the details page (Q-02): observation time, description and
    icon, temperature and feels-like, wind, humidity, pressure, precipitation,
    cloud cover, UV index and visibility. astro and air_quality are optional, so
    the page must render without them (FR-12).

  A few other points are pinned down:
  - Create order: validate → duplicate check → weather call → region check →
    save. A failure at any step saves nothing.
  - Weatherstack errors: each error case maps to one of our codes, the call has
    a 5 s timeout and is never retried, and the key is always redacted.
  - Paging: sorting breaks ties on createdAt by id, so pages don't overlap or
    skip records.
  - Duplicates: two simultaneous creates of the same address store one record;
    the database's unique constraint guarantees this, not just the check before
    the weather call.

  The self-check passes. Weatherstack's error codes (101/104/105/615) and the
